# Pandora Principles

## Own It

- **Get Things Done:** We solve problems end to end and go outside of our scope to “get things done.”
- **Think Long Term:** We always take the decision that is best for our business above our own interests.
- **Use Resources Carefully:** We operate with lean teams and are mindful about how we use our resources.

### Exemplars
- IC3: Manages projects autonomously. Seeks frugal and efficient solutions. Takes responsibility for complex problems in their area.
- IC4: Proposes innovative solutions with significant impact. Manages resources with long-term impact. Prioritizing business over individual area interests.

## Dive Deep

- **Challenge the Data:** We go beyond averages and are skeptical when metrics and anecdotes differ.
- **Know How Things Work:** We understand how things work.
- **Be Hands-On:** We roll up our sleeves and engage at every level.

### Exemplars
- IC3: Challenges data and seeks deep understanding. Collaborates comfortably from detail to broader level. Connects work impact to related areas.
- IC4: Researches internal and external issues. Specific and in-depth discipline knowledge. Involved in critical stages to ensure quality.

## Deliver Value Fast

- **Focus on What Matters:** We relentlessly prioritize based on what our Consumers, Riders & Partners need.
- **Move with Pace:** We move with pace to drive impact.
- **Embrace change:** We adapt quickly and embrace change.

### Exemplars
- IC3: Quickly moves to implement solutions. Prioritizes based on ecosystem value. Iterates on existing solutions.
- IC4: Turns ideas into actionable solutions. Knows when to say 'no' to non-strategic goals. Fostering flexibility and improvement.

## Raise the Bar

- **Set a High Standard:** We set ambitious goals.
- **Hire & Develop the Best:** We build high-performing teams.
- **Provide Actionable Feedback:** We provide constructive feedback.

### Exemplars
- IC3: Collaborates effectively with other areas. Maintains resilient attitude, inspiring others. Celebrating team's achievements.
- IC4: Builds trusting relationships with stakeholders. Demonstrates 'can-do' attitude. Mediates conflicts and promotes positive environment.

## Bring Good Vibes

- **Be a good Team Player:** We act with empathy and consider our impact on others.
- **Focus on What’s Possible:** We focus on what’s possible even in tough times.
- **Create a Positive Workplace:** We create a positive environment and celebrate each other’s success.

### Exemplars
- IC3: Sets ambitious goals in their projects. Provides constructive feedback to peers. Sharing deep expertise to build team.
- IC4: Generates innovative solutions that raise standards. Attracting and developing talent for initiatives. Feedback to colleagues and leaders for growth.

## Stay Humble 

- **Open-mindedness:** We have no ego and are open to challenge our opinions.
- **No Entitlement:** We don’t take things for granted and reject entitlement.
- **Continuous Improvement:** We’re never done learning and always seek to improve ourselves

### Exemplars
- IC3: Challenges own opinions. Analyzes mistakes in structured way. Promotes environment where all voices are heard.
- IC4: Challenges own solutions via diverse perspectives. Shares lessons learned from mistakes. Inviting debate and challenge.
