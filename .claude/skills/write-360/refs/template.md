# write-360 Output Template

## SBI model (peer / 360 feedback)

Third person throughout. SBI is the internal skeleton — not a visible label in the output. One paragraph per observation.

- **Situation**: the specific project, moment, or context where the behaviour occurred
- **Behaviour**: what the person did or said — verifiable actions, not traits or intentions
- **Impact**: effect on the team, customer, or product; anchor to a Pandora principle where it fits naturally (show it, don't label it)

---

## Strength exemplar

> During the Vendor Hub checkout redesign in Q1, the PM escalated a recurring blocker: late-stage scope changes were costing the squad roughly two sprint-days per cycle. [Name] proposed a brief-and-align ritual — a written design brief shared 48 hours before kickoff, followed by a 30-minute sync with the PM and tech lead. [They] authored the brief template and piloted it across four consecutive sprints, tracking scope changes at each one. Late-stage revisions dropped to zero over the pilot, and the PM adopted it as a standing squad practice.

---

## Constructive exemplar

Framing rule: acknowledge what the person did well → name the specific gap → open a forward path with "even more" or "even better".

> In Q2 design critiques for the loyalty programme, [Name] consistently brought high-quality, well-considered visual work to the room. When challenged on trade-offs, however, the rationale behind key decisions wasn't always surfaced — reviewers had to ask follow-up questions to understand why one approach was chosen over another. [Name]'s work would land even more effectively, and accelerate alignment with PMs and engineers, by attaching a brief decision log to each critique deck that names the options considered, the user rationale, and the trade-off accepted.

## Pandora principles — anchor reference

Show the principle through the Impact sentence; never name it as a label ("they demonstrated Dive Deep").

| Principle | Anchor when the behaviour shows… |
|-----------|----------------------------------|
| Own It | End-to-end problem-solving, resourcefulness, taking responsibility beyond their scope |
| Dive Deep | Data-driven decisions, hands-on involvement, challenging assumptions |
| Deliver Value Fast | Prioritisation, pace, iteration with customer focus |
| Raise the Bar | High standards, developing others, giving actionable feedback |
| Bring Good Vibes | Empathy, positive team environment, cross-functional collaboration |
| Stay Humble | Openness to challenge, learning from mistakes, continuous improvement |
